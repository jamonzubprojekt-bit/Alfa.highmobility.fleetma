# Privacy Policy — AREDA Fleet Manager

**Last updated:** 2026-09-02  
**Organisation:** Neuron online, s.r.o.

## 1. Data Controller
Neuron online, s.r.o. is the data controller for the AREDA Fleet Manager platform.

## 2. Data We Collect
AREDA processes **vehicle telemetry data only** (speed, RPM, temperature, battery SOC, odometer). We do **not** collect:
- Driver names or personal identifiers
- License plate numbers
- Personal contact information

All vehicle identifiers are hashed using HMAC-SHA256 before storage (Zero-PII architecture).

## 3. Purpose of Processing
- Fleet maintenance optimization
- Predictive wear analysis
- Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) modeling
- CO₂ emissions reporting (ESG compliance)

## 4. Legal Basis
Processing is based on **legitimate interest** (fleet management optimization) and **contractual necessity** (service delivery to fleet operators).

## 5. Data Retention
Telemetry data is retained for the duration of the fleet lease contract + 24 months for historical analysis. Aggregated statistics are retained indefinitely.

## 6. Third-Party Sharing
We use **High Mobility GmbH** as our telematics data provider. Data is processed under GDPR-compliant Data Processing Agreement (DPA).

## 7. Your Rights
Under GDPR, you have the right to:
- Access your data
- Request correction or deletion
- Object to processing
- Data portability

Contact: [your-email@neuron-online.cz]

## 8. Security
All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest. Access is controlled via role-based authentication.
